Global IEEE Footprint

Global Vision

IEEE will be essential to the global technical community and to technical professionals everywhere, and be universally recognized for the contributions of technology and of technical professionals in improving global conditions.

Global Mission

IEEE’s core purpose is to foster technological innovation and excellence for the benefit of humanity. We inspire a global community through highly-cited publications, conferences, technology standards, and professional development.

IEEE PCE Student Branch

Established in 2005 at Pillai College of Engineering (New Panvel), the IEEE PCE Student Branch was built on a vision to give students a foundational understanding of technical concepts and nurture their innate curiosity in technology.

Operating under the Bombay Section, IEEE PCE provides a dynamic platform where budding engineers step beyond classroom academia. From domain-specific coding hackathons like Cognithon to interactive technical competitions like Code Autopsy, our initiatives equip students with practical problem-solving capabilities.

Our vibrant ecosystem encourages collaborative growth, professional networking, research publication opportunities, and peer-to-peer mentorship across every engineering discipline.
